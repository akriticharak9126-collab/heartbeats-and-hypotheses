BEHIND THE HYPOTHESES

A little about me

I'm Akriti Charak.

A zoology student, an independent science writer and, above all, a curious observer of the world.

I've always been fascinated by the stories hidden within science — the extraordinary lives of organisms, the questions that challenge what we know, and the discoveries that change how we see the world.

Through Heartbeats & Hypotheses, I hope to explore scientific ideas beyond textbooks, understand research in my own way, and make complex discoveries feel accessible, meaningful and human.

This is a space for curiosity, thoughtful questions and the beautiful intersection of science and life.

Welcome to my little corner of the scientific world.

— Akriti
